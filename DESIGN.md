# Design direction

Greenfield developer portfolio for readers assessing Sreehan's projects, research, and interests. Custom editorial aesthetic, not an implementation of a commercial design system.

Design variance: 8. Motion intensity: 6. Visual density: 3.

- **monopo saigon:** generous whitespace, large light-weight sans typography, patient transitions, sharp image frames, pill controls, and one iridescent visual gesture.
- **dope.security:** warm midnight canvas, lavender signals for interaction, restrained frosted navigation, and mono labels for technical context.
- **ORYZO AI:** isolated sculptural object with real material depth, cream foreground, editorial product presentation, and dashed section divisions.

These are combined with one semantic palette throughout each theme. Lavender is the single interface accent. Warm/copper/sage reflections remain within the hero image. The whole page changes together when the theme is toggled. Image frames and containers are sharp; interactive controls are pills. Product media retains its original colors.

The hero's pointer response lets visitors explore the sculpture's perspective and light. Project filters reduce the collection to a reader's interest, dialogs reveal real technical detail, and accordions keep background information scannable. Motion respects the system preference and can be paused. Native scrolling and pointer behavior remain intact.

Fonts: self-hosted Manrope and IBM Plex Mono, with their OFL licenses in `dist/assets/`. The sculpture is an original image generated with the built-in image generation tool. Final asset: `dist/assets/sculpture.jpg`.

Image prompt: A single sculptural continuous loop made from polished liquid chrome and smoked glass, folded like a soft torus knot, isolated floating in a warm near-black void. Museum object photography, subtle violet, sage, amber, and copper reflections, one lavender rim light, warm cream highlights, no text, no stars, no grid, no watermark. Landscape composition for a developer portfolio hero.
